About Tembisa Animal Rescue

Our Mission

Founded in 2018 to rescue, rehabilitate, and rehome abandoned and abused animals in Tembisa. Every stray deserves a second chance. We provide shelter, veterinary care, sterilization, and adoption services. Our motto: Adopt Don't Shop!

Vision: A Tembisa where no animal suffers on the streets.

Our Team

Bhekizwe Ndlovu - Founder & Rescue Manager - Passionate about animal welfare since 2015.

Team Members: 5 Volunteers, 2 Vet Nurses, 10 Dog Walkers. We rescue 20+ animals monthly in Gauteng.

Located: 123 Rescue Street, Tembisa, 1632. We serve entire Tembisa and Ivory Park.

Our Impact

✅ 500+ Animals Rescued since 2018

✅ 350+ Successful Adoptions in Gauteng

✅ 1000+ Sterilizations to control overpopulation

✅ Community Education - Weekly outreach in Tembisa schools.
